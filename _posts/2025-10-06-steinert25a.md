abstract: We study the incorporation of equivariances into vector-valued GPs and more
  general classes of random field models. While kernels guaranteeing equivariances
  have been investigated previously, their evaluation is often computationally prohibitive
  due to required integrations over the involved groups. In this work, we provide
  a kernel characterization of stochastic equivariance for centred second-order vector-valued
  random fields and we construct integration-free equivariant kernels based on the
  notion of fundamental regions of group actions. We establish data-efficient and
  computationally lightweight GP models for velocity fields and molecular electric
  dipole moments and demonstrate that proposed integration-free kernels may also be
  leveraged to extract equivariant components from data.
