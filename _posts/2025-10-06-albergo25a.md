title: 'NETS: A Non-equilibrium Transport Sampler'

abstract: We introduce the Non-Equilibrium Transport Sampler (NETS), an algorithm
  for sampling from unnormalized probability distributions. NETS builds on non-equilibrium
  sampling strategies that transport a simple base distribution into the target distribution
  in finite time, as pioneered in Neal’s annealed importance sampling (AIS). In the
  continuous-time setting, this transport is accomplished by evolving walkers using
  Langevin dynamics with a time-dependent potential, while simultaneously evolving
  importance weights to debias their solutions following Jarzynski’s equality. The
  key innovation of NETS is to add to the dynamics a learned drift term that offsets
  the need for these corrective weights by minimizing their variance through an objective
  that can be estimated without backpropagation and provably bounds the Kullback-Leibler
  divergence between the estimated and target distributions. NETS provides unbiased
  samples and features a tunable diffusion coefficient that can be adjusted after
  training to maximize the effective sample size. In experiments on standard benchmarks,
  high-dimensional Gaussian mixtures, and statistical lattice field theory models,
  NETS shows compelling performances.
