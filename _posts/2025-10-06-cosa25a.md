title: Learning-Augmented Algorithms for MTS with Bandit Access to Multiple Predictors

abstract: 'Combining algorithms is one of the key techniques in learning-augmented
  algorithms. We consider the following problem: We are given $\ell$ heuristics for
  Metrical Task Systems (MTS), where each might be tailored to a different type of
  input instances. While processing an input instance received online, we are allowed
  to query the action of only one of the heuristics at each time step. Our goal is
  to achieve performance comparable to the best of the given heuristics. The main
  difficulty of our setting comes from the fact that the cost paid by a heuristic
  at time $t$ cannot be estimated unless the same heuristic was also queried at time
  $t-1$. This is related to Bandit Learning against memory bounded adversaries (Arora
  et al., 2012). We show how to achieve regret of $O(\text{OPT}^{2/3})$ and prove
  a tight lower bound based on the construction of Dekel et al. (2013).'
