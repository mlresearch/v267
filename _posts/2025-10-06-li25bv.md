abstract: Despite their remarkable capabilities, large language models often struggle
  with tasks requiring complex reasoning and planning. While existing approaches like
  Chain-of-Thought prompting and tree search techniques show promise, they are limited
  by their reliance on predefined heuristics and computationally expensive exploration
  strategies. We propose Policy-Guided Tree Search (PGTS), a framework that combines
  reinforcement learning with structured tree exploration to efficiently navigate
  reasoning paths. Our key innovation is a learned policy that dynamically decides
  between expanding, branching, backtracking, or terminating exploration, eliminating
  the need for manual heuristics or exhaustive search. Experiments across mathematical
  reasoning, logical deduction, and planning benchmarks demonstrate that PGTS achieves
  superior reasoning performance while significantly reducing computational costs
  compared to existing methods. These results establish PGTS as a scalable and effective
  solution for tackling complex reasoning tasks with LLMs.
