abstract: Existing methods for inferring latent relational structures struggle to
  integrate partial prior knowledge, such as known edges, node-degree constraints,
  and global sparsity, without destabilizing training or conflicting with probabilistic
  assumptions. We propose Soft-Gated Structural Inference (SGSI), a VAE framework
  that seamlessly incorporates domain constraints via (1) soft gating with learnable
  edge masks to preserve gradients, (2) cloning-clamping of deterministic edges to
  avoid distributional conflicts, and (3) adaptive regularization to balance data-driven
  learning with domain constraints. By excluding known edges from stochastic inference,
  SGSI reallocates capacity to uncertain interactions, optimizing the information
  bottleneck trade-off. Experiments on 16 datasets show SGSI improves edge recovery
  by up to $9$% AUROC over baselines, scales to larger graphs ($94.2$% AUROC), and
  maintains stable training. SGSI bridges domain expertise with data-driven learning,
  enabling interpretable and robust structural discovery in dynamical systems.
