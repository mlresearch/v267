title: 'Ensemble Learned Bloom Filters: Two Oracles are Better than One'

abstract: Bloom filters (BF) are space-efficient probabilistic data structures for
  approximate membership testing. Boosted by the proliferation of machine learning,
  learned Bloom filters (LBF) were recently proposed by augmenting the canonical BFs
  with a learned oracle as a pre-filter, the size of which is crucial to the compactness
  of the overall system. In this paper, inspired by ensemble learning, we depart from
  the state-of-the-art single-oracle LBF structure by demonstrating that, by leveraging
  multiple learning oracles of smaller size and carefully optimizing the accompanied
  backup filters, we can significantly boost the performance of LBF under the same
  space budget. We then design and optimize ensemble learned Bloom filters for mutually
  independent and correlated learning oracles respectively. We also empirically demonstrate
  the performance improvement of our propositions under three practical data analysis
  tasks.
