title: Calibrated Value-Aware Model Learning with Probabilistic Environment Models

abstract: The idea of value-aware model learning, that models should produce accurate
  value estimates, has gained prominence in model-based reinforcement learning. The
  MuZero loss, which penalizes a model’s value function prediction compared to the
  ground-truth value function, has been utilized in several prominent empirical works
  in the literature. However, theoretical investigation into its strengths and weaknesses
  is limited. In this paper, we analyze the family of value-aware model learning losses,
  which includes the popular MuZero loss. We show that these losses, as normally used,
  are uncalibrated surrogate losses, which means that they do not always recover the
  correct model and value function. Building on this insight, we propose corrections
  to solve this issue. Furthermore, we investigate the interplay between the loss
  calibration, latent model architectures, and auxiliary losses that are commonly
  employed when training MuZero-style agents. We show that while deterministic models
  can be sufficient to predict accurate values, learning calibrated stochastic models
  is still advantageous.
