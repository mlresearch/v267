abstract: 'We propose DRAGO, a novel approach for continual model-based reinforcement
  learning aimed at improving the incremental development of world models across a
  sequence of tasks that differ in their reward functions but not the state space
  or dynamics. DRAGO comprises two key components: <em>Synthetic Experience Rehearsal</em>,
  which leverages generative models to create synthetic experiences from past tasks,
  allowing the agent to reinforce previously learned dynamics without storing data,
  and <em>Regaining Memories Through Exploration</em>, which introduces an intrinsic
  reward mechanism to guide the agent toward revisiting relevant states from prior
  tasks. Together, these components enable the agent to maintain a comprehensive and
  continually developing world model, facilitating more effective learning and adaptation
  across diverse environments. Empirical evaluations demonstrate that DRAGO is able
  to preserve knowledge across tasks, achieving superior performance in various continual
  learning scenarios.'
