title: Zero Shot Generalization of Vision-Based RL Without Data Augmentation
openreview: kQ42hGjrKn
abstract: Generalizing vision-based reinforcement learning (RL) agents to novel environments
  remains a difficult and open challenge. Current trends are to collect large-scale
  datasets or use data augmentation techniques to prevent overfitting and improve
  downstream generalization. However, the computational and data collection costs
  increase exponentially with the number of task variations and can destabilize the
  already difficult task of training RL agents. In this work, we take inspiration
  from recent advances in computational neuroscience and propose a model, Associative
  Latent DisentAnglement (ALDA), that builds on standard off-policy RL towards zero-shot
  generalization. Specifically, we revisit the role of latent disentanglement in RL
  and show how combining it with a model of associative memory achieves zero-shot
  generalization on difficult task variations <em>without</em> relying on data augmentation.
  Finally, we formally show that data augmentation techniques are a form of weak disentanglement
  and discuss the implications of this insight.
