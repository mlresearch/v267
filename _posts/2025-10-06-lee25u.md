abstract: Scaling up the model size and computation has brought consistent performance
  improvements in supervised learning. However, this lesson often fails to apply to
  reinforcement learning (RL) because training the model on non-stationary data easily
  leads to overfitting and unstable optimization. In response, we introduce SimbaV2,
  a novel RL architecture designed to stabilize optimization by (i) constraining the
  growth of weight and feature norm by hyperspherical normalization; and (ii) using
  a distributional value estimation with reward scaling to maintain stable gradients
  under varying reward magnitudes. Using the soft actor-critic as a base algorithm,
  SimbaV2 scales up effectively with larger models and greater compute, achieving
  state-of-the-art performance on 57 continuous control tasks across 4 domains.
