abstract: '3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) has emerged as a powerful technique for novel
  view synthesis. However, existing methods struggle to adaptively optimize the distribution
  of Gaussian primitives based on scene characteristics, making it challenging to
  balance reconstruction quality and efficiency. Inspired by human perception, we
  propose scene-adaptive perceptual densification for Gaussian Splatting (Perceptual-GS),
  a novel framework that integrates perceptual sensitivity into the 3DGS training
  process to address this challenge. We first introduce a perception-aware representation
  that models human visual sensitivity while constraining the number of Gaussian primitives.
  Building on this foundation, we develop a perceptual sensitivity-adaptive distribution
  to allocate finer Gaussian granularity to visually critical regions, enhancing reconstruction
  quality and robustness. Extensive evaluations on multiple datasets, including BungeeNeRF
  for large-scale scenes, demonstrate that Perceptual-GS achieves state-of-the-art
  performance in reconstruction quality, efficiency, and robustness. The code is publicly
  available at: https://github.com/eezkni/Perceptual-GS'
