title: Regularized Langevin Dynamics for Combinatorial Optimization
openreview: bbJ0QCujU4
abstract: This work proposes a simple yet effective sampling framework for combinatorial
  optimization (CO). Our method builds on discrete Langevin dynamics (LD), an efficient
  gradient-guided generative paradigm. However, we observe that directly applying
  LD often leads to limited exploration. To overcome this limitation, we propose the
  Regularized Langevin Dynamics (RLD), which enforces an expected distance between
  the sampled and current solutions, effectively avoiding local minima. We develop
  two CO solvers on top of RLD, one based on simulated annealing (SA), and the other
  one based on neural network (NN). Empirical results on three classic CO problems
  demonstrate that both of our methods can achieve comparable or better performance
  against the previous state-of-the-art (SOTA) SA- and NN-based solvers. In particular,
  our SA algorithm reduces the runtime of the previous SOTA SA method by up to 80%,
  while achieving equal or superior performance. In summary, RLD offers a promising
  framework for enhancing both traditional heuristics and NN models to solve CO problems.
  Our code is available at https://github.com/Shengyu-Feng/RLD4CO.
