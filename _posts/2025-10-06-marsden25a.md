abstract: We consider the problem of length generalization in sequence prediction.
  We define a new metric of performance in this setting – the Asymmetric-Regret– which
  measures regret against a benchmark predictor with longer context length than available
  to the learner. We continue by studying this concept through the lens of the spectral
  filter-ing algorithm. We present a gradient-based learn-ing algorithm that provably
  achieves length generalization for linear dynamical systems. We conclude with proof-of-concept
  experiments which are consistent with our theory.
