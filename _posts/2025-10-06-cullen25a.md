abstract: While certified robustness is widely promoted as a solution to adversarial
  examples in Artificial Intelligence systems, significant challenges remain before
  these techniques can be meaningfully deployed in real-world applications. We identify
  critical gaps in current research, including the paradox of detection without distinction,
  the lack of clear criteria for practitioners to evaluate certification schemes,
  and the potential security risks arising from users’ expectations surrounding “guaranteed"
  robustness claims. These create an alignment issue between how certifications are
  presented and perceived, relative to their actual capabilities. This position paper
  is a call to arms for the certification research community, proposing concrete steps
  to address these fundamental challenges and advance the field toward practical applicability.
