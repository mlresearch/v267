abstract: As deep learning models are increasingly deployed in high-risk applications,
  robust defenses against adversarial attacks and reliable performance guarantees
  become paramount. Moreover, accuracy alone does not provide sufficient assurance
  or reliable uncertainty estimates for these models. This study advances adversarial
  training by leveraging principles from Conformal Prediction. Specifically, we develop
  an adversarial attack method, termed OPSA (OPtimal Size Attack), designed to reduce
  the efficiency of conformal prediction at any significance level by maximizing model
  uncertainty without requiring coverage guarantees. Correspondingly, we introduce
  OPSA-AT (Adversarial Training), a defense strategy that integrates OPSA within a
  novel conformal training paradigm. Experimental evaluations demonstrate that our
  OPSA attack method induces greater uncertainty compared to baseline approaches for
  various defenses. Conversely, our OPSA-AT defensive model significantly enhances
  robustness not only against OPSA but also other adversarial attacks, and maintains
  reliable prediction. Our findings highlight the effectiveness of this integrated
  approach for developing trustworthy and resilient deep learning models for safety-critical
  domains. Our code is available at https://github.com/bjbbbb/Enhancing-Adversarial-Robustness-with-Conformal-Prediction.
