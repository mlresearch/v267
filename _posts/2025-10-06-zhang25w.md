title: 'UP-VLA: A Unified Understanding and Prediction Model for Embodied Agent'

abstract: Recent advancements in Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models have leveraged
  pre-trained Vision-Language Models (VLMs) to improve the generalization capabilities.
  VLMs, typically pre-trained on vision-language understanding tasks, provide rich
  semantic knowledge and reasoning abilities. However, prior research has shown that
  VLMs often focus on high-level semantic content and neglect low-level features,
  limiting their ability to capture detailed spatial information and understand physical
  dynamics. These aspects, which are crucial for embodied control tasks, remain underexplored
  in existing pre-training paradigms. In this paper, we investigate the training paradigm
  for VLAs, and introduce <b>UP-VLA</b>, a <b>U</b>nified VLA model training with
  both multi-modal <b>U</b>nderstanding and future <b>P</b>rediction objectives, enhancing
  both high-level semantic comprehension and low-level spatial understanding. Experimental
  results show that UP-VLA achieves a 33% improvement on the Calvin ABC-D benchmark
  compared to the previous state-of-the-art method. Additionally, UP-VLA demonstrates
  improved success rates in real-world manipulation tasks, particularly those requiring
  precise spatial information.
