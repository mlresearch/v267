title: Distribution-aware Fairness Learning in Medical Image Segmentation From A Control-Theoretic

abstract: Ensuring fairness in medical image segmentation is critical due to biases
  in imbalanced clinical data acquisition caused by demographic attributes (e.g.,
  age, sex, race) and clinical factors (e.g., disease severity). To address these
  challenges, we introduce Distribution-aware Mixture of Experts (dMoE), inspired
  by optimal control theory. We provide a comprehensive analysis of its underlying
  mechanisms and clarify dMoE’s role in adapting to heterogeneous distributions in
  medical image segmentation. Furthermore, we integrate dMoE into multiple network
  architectures, demonstrating its broad applicability across diverse medical image
  analysis tasks. By incorporating demographic and clinical factors, dMoE achieves
  state-of-the-art performance on two 2D benchmark datasets and a 3D in-house dataset.
  Our results highlight the effectiveness of dMoE in mitigating biases from imbalanced
  distributions, offering a promising approach to bridging control theory and medical
  image segmentation within fairness learning paradigms. The source code is available
  at https://github.com/tvseg/dMoE.
