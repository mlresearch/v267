abstract: 'Large Language Models (LLMs) exhibit strong potential in mathematical reasoning,
  yet their effectiveness is often limited by a shortage of high-quality queries.
  This limitation necessitates scaling up computational responses through self-generated
  data, yet current methods struggle due to spurious correlated data caused by ineffective
  exploration across all reasoning stages. To address such challenge, we introduce
  <b>MARGE</b>: Improving <b>Ma</b>th <b>R</b>easoning with <b>G</b>uided <b>E</b>xploration,
  a novel method that enhances mathematical reasoning through hit-guided exploration.
  MARGE systematically explores intermediate reasoning states derived from self-generated
  solutions, enabling adequate exploration and improved credit assignment throughout
  the reasoning process. Notably, MARGE improves both single-shot accuracy and exploration
  diversity, mitigating a common trade-off in alignment methods. These results demonstrate
  MARGE’s effectiveness in enhancing mathematical reasoning capabilities and unlocking
  the potential of scaling self-generated training data.'
