title: 'BARK: A Fully Bayesian Tree Kernel for Black-box Optimization'

abstract: We perform Bayesian optimization using a Gaussian process perspective on
  Bayesian Additive Regression Trees (BART). Our BART Kernel (BARK) uses tree agreement
  to define a posterior over piecewise-constant functions, and we explore the space
  of tree kernels using a Markov chain Monte Carlo approach. Where BART only samples
  functions, the resulting BARK model obtains samples of Gaussian processes defining
  distributions over functions, which allow us to build acquisition functions for
  Bayesian optimization. Our tree-based approach enables global optimization over
  the surrogate, even for mixed-feature spaces. Moreover, where many previous tree-based
  kernels provide uncertainty quantification over function values, our sampling scheme
  captures uncertainty over the tree structure itself. Our experiments show the strong
  performance of BARK on both synthetic and applied benchmarks, due to the combination
  of our fully Bayesian surrogate and the optimization procedure.
