title: Towards Attributions of Input Variables in a Coalition

abstract: This paper focuses on the fundamental challenge of partitioning input variables
  in attribution methods for Explainable AI, particularly in Shapley value-based approaches.
  Previous methods always compute attributions given a predefined partition but lack
  theoretical guidance on how to form meaningful variable partitions. We identify
  that attribution conflicts arise when the attribution of a coalition differs from
  the sum of its individual variables’ attributions. To address this, we analyze the
  numerical effects of AND-OR interactions in AI models and extend the Shapley value
  to a new attribution metric for variable coalitions. Our theoretical findings reveal
  that specific interactions cause attribution conflicts, and we propose three metrics
  to evaluate coalition faithfulness. Experiments on synthetic data, NLP, image classification,
  and the game of Go validate our approach, demonstrating consistency with human intuition
  and practical applicability.
