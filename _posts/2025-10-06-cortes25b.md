abstract: We introduce $\texttt{TimeMCL}$, a method leveraging the Multiple Choice
  Learning (MCL) paradigm to forecast multiple plausible time series futures. Our
  approach employs a neural network with multiple heads and utilizes the Winner-Takes-All
  (WTA) loss to promote diversity among predictions. MCL has recently gained attention
  due to its simplicity and ability to address ill-posed and ambiguous tasks. We propose
  an adaptation of this framework for time-series forecasting, presenting it as an
  efficient method to predict diverse futures, which we relate to its implicit <em>quantization</em>
  objective. We provide insights into our approach using synthetic data and evaluate
  it on real-world time series, demonstrating its promising performance at a light
  computational cost.
