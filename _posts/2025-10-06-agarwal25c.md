abstract: Federated learning harnesses the power of distributed optimization to train
  a unified machine learning model across separate clients. However, heterogeneous
  data distributions and computational workloads can lead to inconsistent updates
  and limit model performance. This work tackles these challenges by proposing FedECADO,
  a new algorithm inspired by a dynamical system representation of the federated learning
  process. FedECADO addresses non-IID data distribution through an aggregate sensitivity
  model that reflects the amount of data processed by each client. To tackle heterogeneous
  computing, we design a multi-rate integration method with adaptive step-size selections
  that synchronizes active client updates in continuous time. Compared to prominent
  techniques, including FedProx, FedExp, and FedNova, FedECADO achieves higher classification
  accuracies in numerous heterogeneous scenarios.
