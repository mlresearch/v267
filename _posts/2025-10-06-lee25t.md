title: Fluctuations of the largest eigenvalues of transformed spiked Wigner matrices
openreview: xraEFvFHR4
abstract: We consider a spiked random matrix model obtained by applying a function
  entrywise to a signal-plus-noise symmetric data matrix. We prove that the largest
  eigenvalue of this model, which we call a transformed spiked Wigner matrix, exhibits
  Baik-Ben Arous-Péché (BBP) type phase transition. We show that the law of the fluctuation
  converges to the Gaussian distribution when the effective signal-to-noise ratio
  (SNR) is above the critical number, and to the GOE Tracy-Widom distribution when
  the effective SNR is below the critical number. We provide precise formulas for
  the limiting distributions and also concentration estimates for the largest eigenvalues,
  both in the supercritical and the subcritical regimes.
