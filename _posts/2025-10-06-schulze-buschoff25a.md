abstract: Pre-trained vision language models still fall short of human visual cognition.
  In an effort to improve visual cognition and align models with human behavior, we
  introduce visual stimuli and human judgments on visual cognition tasks, allowing
  us to systematically evaluate performance across cognitive domains under a consistent
  environment. We fine-tune models on ground truth data for intuitive physics and
  causal reasoning and find that this improves model performance in the respective
  fine-tuning domain. Furthermore, it can improve model alignment with human behavior.
  However, we find that task-specific fine-tuning does not contribute to robust human-like
  generalization to data with other visual characteristics or to tasks in other cognitive
  domains.
