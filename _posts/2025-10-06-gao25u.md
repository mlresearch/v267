title: 'AdaWorld: Learning Adaptable World Models with Latent Actions'
openreview: QQegZj99sk
abstract: World models aim to learn action-controlled future prediction and have proven
  essential for the development of intelligent agents. However, most existing world
  models rely heavily on substantial action-labeled data and costly training, making
  it challenging to adapt to novel environments with heterogeneous actions through
  limited interactions. This limitation can hinder their applicability across broader
  domains. To overcome this limitation, we propose AdaWorld, an innovative world model
  learning approach that enables efficient adaptation. The key idea is to incorporate
  action information during the pretraining of world models. This is achieved by extracting
  latent actions from videos in a self-supervised manner, capturing the most critical
  transitions between frames. We then develop an autoregressive world model that conditions
  on these latent actions. This learning paradigm enables highly adaptable world models,
  facilitating efficient transfer and learning of new actions even with limited interactions
  and finetuning. Our comprehensive experiments across multiple environments demonstrate
  that AdaWorld achieves superior performance in both simulation quality and visual
  planning.
