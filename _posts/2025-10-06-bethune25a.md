title: Scaling Laws for Forgetting during Finetuning with Pretraining Data Injection
openreview: vWMij23BmQ
abstract: 'A widespread strategy to obtain a language model that performs well on
  a target domain is to finetune a pretrained model to perform unsupervised next-token
  prediction on data from that target domain. Finetuning presents two challenges:
  <em>(i)</em> if the amount of target data is limited, as in most practical applications,
  the model will quickly overfit, and <em>(ii)</em> the model will drift away from
  the original model, forgetting the pretraining data and the generic knowledge that
  comes with it. Our goal is to derive scaling laws that quantify these two phenomena
  for various target domains, amounts of available target data, and model scales.
  We measure the efficiency of injecting pretraining data into the finetuning data
  mixture to avoid forgetting and mitigate overfitting. A key practical takeaway from
  our study is that injecting as little as $1%$ of pretraining data in the finetuning
  data mixture prevents the model from forgetting the pretraining set.'
