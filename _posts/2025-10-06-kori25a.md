abstract: Modular object-centric representations are essential for <em>human-like
  reasoning</em> but are challenging to obtain under spatial ambiguities, <em>e.g.
  due to occlusions and view ambiguities</em>. However, addressing challenges presents
  both theoretical and practical difficulties. We introduce a novel multi-view probabilistic
  approach that aggregates view-specific slots to capture <em>invariant content</em>
  information while simultaneously learning disentangled global <em>viewpoint-level</em>
  information. Unlike prior single-view methods, our approach resolves spatial ambiguities,
  provides theoretical guarantees for identifiability, and requires <em>no viewpoint
  annotations</em>. Extensive experiments on standard benchmarks and novel complex
  datasets validate our method’s robustness and scalability.
