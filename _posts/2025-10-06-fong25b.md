title: Pareto-Optimal Fronts for Benchmarking Symbolic Regression Algorithms
openreview: mvbWw0w7pG
abstract: 'Symbolic Regression (SR) algorithms select expressions based on prediction
  performance while also keeping the expression lengths short to produce explainable
  white box models. In this context, SR algorithms can be evaluated by measuring the
  extent to which the expressions discovered are Pareto-optimal, in the sense of having
  the best R-squared score for a given expression length. This evaluation is most
  commonly done based on relative performance, in the sense that an SR algorithm is
  judged on whether it Pareto-dominates other SR algorithms selected in the analysis,
  without any indication on efficiency or attainable limits. In this paper, we explore
  absolute Pareto-optimal (APO) solutions instead, which have the optimal tradeoff
  between the multiple SR objectives, for 34 datasets in the widely-used SR benchmark,
  SRBench, by performing exhaustive search. Additionally, we include comparisons between
  eight numerical optimization methods. We extract, for every dataset, an APO front
  of expressions that can serve as a universal baseline for SR algorithms that informs
  researchers of the best attainable performance for selected sizes. The APO fronts
  provided serves as an important benchmark and performance limit for SR algorithms
  and is made publicly available at: https://github.com/kentridgeai/SRParetoFronts'
