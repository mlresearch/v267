abstract: 'We present MILS: Multimodal Iterative LLM Solver, a surprisingly simple,
  training-free approach, to imbue multimodal capabilities into your favorite LLM.
  Leveraging their innate ability to perform multi-step reasoning, MILS prompts the
  LLM to generate candidate outputs, each of which are scored and fed back iteratively,
  eventually generating a solution to the task. This enables various applications
  that typically require training specialized models on task-specific data. In particular,
  we establish a new state-of-the-art on emergent zero-shot image, video and audio
  captioning. MILS seamlessly applies to media generation as well, discovering prompt
  rewrites to improve text-to-image generation, and even edit prompts for style transfer!
  Finally, being a gradient-free optimization approach, MILS can invert multimodal
  embeddings into text, enabling applications like cross-modal arithmetic.'
