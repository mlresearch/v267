abstract: Model immunization aims to pre-train models that are difficult to fine-tune
  on harmful tasks while retaining their utility on other non-harmful tasks. Though
  prior work has shown empirical evidence for immunizing text-to-image models, the
  key understanding of when immunization is possible and a precise definition of an
  immunized model remain unclear. In this work, we propose a framework, based on the
  condition number of a Hessian matrix, to analyze model immunization for linear models.
  Building on this framework, we design an algorithm with regularization terms to
  control the resulting condition numbers after pre-training. Empirical results on
  linear models and non-linear deep-nets demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed
  algorithm on model immunization. The code is available at https://github.com/amberyzheng/model-immunization-cond-num.
