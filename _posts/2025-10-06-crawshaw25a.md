abstract: Existing analysis of Local (Stochastic) Gradient Descent for heterogeneous
  objectives requires stepsizes $\eta \leq 1/K$ where $K$ is the communication interval,
  which ensures monotonic decrease of the objective. In contrast, we analyze Local
  Gradient Descent for logistic regression with separable, heterogeneous data using
  any stepsize $\eta > 0$. With $R$ communication rounds and $M$ clients, we show
  convergence at a rate $\mathcal{O}(1/\eta K R)$ after an initial unstable phase
  lasting for $\widetilde{\mathcal{O}}(\eta K M)$ rounds. This improves upon the existing
  $\mathcal{O}(1/R)$ rate for general smooth, convex objectives. Our analysis parallels
  the single machine analysis of Wu et al. (2024) in which instability is caused by
  extremely large stepsizes, but in our setting another source of instability is large
  local updates with heterogeneous objectives.
