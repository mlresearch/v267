abstract: Decentralized learning has emerged as a powerful approach for handling large
  datasets across multiple machines in a communication-efficient manner. However,
  such methods often face scalability limitations, as increasing the number of machines
  beyond a certain point negatively impacts convergence rates. In this work, we propose
  <em>Decentralized Anytime SGD</em>, a novel decentralized learning algorithm that
  significantly extends the critical parallelism threshold, enabling the effective
  use of more machines without compromising performance. Within the stochastic convex
  optimization (SCO) framework, we establish a theoretical upper bound on parallelism
  that surpasses the current state-of-the-art, allowing larger networks to achieve
  favorable statistical guarantees and closing the gap with centralized learning in
  highly connected topologies.
