abstract: In this paper, we investigate the existence of online learning algorithms
  with bandit feedback that simultaneously guarantee $O(1)$ regret compared to a given
  comparator strategy, and $\tilde{O}(\sqrt{T})$ regret compared to any fixed strategy,
  where $T$ is the number of rounds. We provide the first affirmative answer to this
  question whenever the comparator strategy supports every action. In the context
  of zero-sum games with min-max value zero, both in normal- and extensive form, we
  show that our results allow us to guarantee to risk at most $O(1)$ loss while being
  able to gain $\Omega(T)$ from exploitable opponents, thereby combining the benefits
  of both no-regret algorithms and minimax play.
