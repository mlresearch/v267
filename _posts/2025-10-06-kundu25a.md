abstract: We introduce Steerable Transformers, an extension of the Vision Transformer
  mechanism that maintains equivariance to the special Euclidean group $\mathrm{SE}(d)$.
  We propose an equivariant attention mechanism that operates on features extracted
  by steerable convolutions. Operating in Fourier space, our network utilizes Fourier
  space non-linearities. Our experiments in both two and three dimensions show that
  adding steerable transformer layers to steerable convolutional networks enhances
  performance.
