abstract: We propose Electrostatic Field Matching (EFM), a novel method that is suitable
  for both generative modelling and distribution transfer tasks. Our approach is inspired
  by the physics of an electrical capacitor. We place source and target distributions
  on the capacitor plates and assign them positive and negative charges, respectively.
  We then learn the capacitor’s electrostatic field using a neural network approximator.
  To map the distributions to each other, we start at one plate of the capacitor and
  move the samples along the learned electrostatic field lines until they reach the
  other plate. We theoretically justify that this approach provably yields the distribution
  transfer. In practice, we demonstrate the performance of our EFM in toy and image
  data experiments.
