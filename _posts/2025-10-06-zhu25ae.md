abstract: Large Language Models (LLMs) struggle with complex reasoning due to limited
  diversity and inefficient search. We propose Soft Reasoning, an embedding-based
  search framework that optimises the embedding of the first token to guide generation.
  It combines (1) embedding perturbation for controlled exploration and (2) Bayesian
  optimisation to refine embeddings via a verifier-guided objective, balancing exploration
  and exploitation. This approach improves reasoning accuracy and coherence while
  avoiding reliance on heuristic search. Experiments demonstrate superior correctness
  with minimal computation, making it a scalable, model-agnostic solution.
