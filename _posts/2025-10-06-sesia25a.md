abstract: We present a conformal inference method for constructing lower prediction
  bounds for survival times from right-censored data, extending recent approaches
  designed for more restrictive type-I censoring scenarios. The proposed method imputes
  unobserved censoring times using a machine learning model, and then analyzes the
  imputed data using a survival model calibrated via weighted conformal inference.
  This approach is theoretically supported by an asymptotic double robustness property.
  Empirical studies on simulated and real data demonstrate that our method leads to
  relatively informative predictive inferences and is especially robust in challenging
  settings where the survival model may be inaccurate.
