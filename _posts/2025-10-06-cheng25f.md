title: Accelerating PDE-Constrained Optimization by the Derivative of Neural Operators
openreview: LFF7kUQ5Rp
abstract: 'PDE-Constrained Optimization (PDECO) problems can be accelerated significantly
  by employing gradient-based methods with surrogate models like neural operators
  compared to traditional numerical solvers. However, this approach faces two key
  challenges: (1) <b>Data inefficiency</b>: Lack of efficient data sampling and effective
  training for neural operators, particularly for optimization purpose. (2) <b>Instability</b>:
  High risk of optimization derailment due to inaccurate neural operator predictions
  and gradients. To address these challenges, we propose a novel framework: (1) <b>Optimization-oriented
  training</b>: we leverage data from full steps of traditional optimization algorithms
  and employ a specialized training method for neural operators. (2) <b>Enhanced derivative
  learning</b>: We introduce a <b>Virtual-Fourier</b> layer to enhance derivative
  learning within the neural operator, a crucial aspect for gradient-based optimization.
  (3) <b>Hybrid optimization</b>: We implement a hybrid approach that integrates neural
  operators with numerical solvers, providing robust regularization for the optimization
  process. Our extensive experimental results demonstrate the effectiveness of our
  model in accurately learning operators and their derivatives. Furthermore, our hybrid
  optimization approach exhibits robust convergence.'
