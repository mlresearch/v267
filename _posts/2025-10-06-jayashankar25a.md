abstract: We propose <em>Score-of-Mixture Training</em> (SMT), a novel framework for
  training one-step generative models by minimizing a class of divergences called
  the $\alpha$-skew Jensen–Shannon divergence. At its core, SMT estimates the score
  of mixture distributions between real and fake samples across multiple noise levels.
  Similar to consistency models, our approach supports both training from scratch
  (SMT) and distillation using a pretrained diffusion model, which we call <em>Score-of-Mixture
  Distillation</em> (SMD). It is simple to implement, requires minimal hyperparameter
  tuning, and ensures stable training. Experiments on CIFAR-10 and ImageNet 64$\times$64
  show that SMT/SMD are competitive with and can even outperform existing methods.
