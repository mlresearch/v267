abstract: Trajectory prediction for surrounding agents is a challenging task in autonomous
  driving due to its inherent uncertainty and underlying multimodality. Unlike prevailing
  data-driven methods that primarily rely on supervised learning, in this paper, we
  introduce a novel <b>G</b>raph-<b>o</b>riented <b>I</b>nverse <b>R</b>einforcement
  <b>L</b>earning (GoIRL) framework, which is an IRL-based predictor equipped with
  vectorized context representations. We develop a feature adaptor to effectively
  aggregate lane-graph features into grid space, enabling seamless integration with
  the maximum entropy IRL paradigm to infer the reward distribution and obtain the
  policy that can be sampled to induce multiple plausible plans. Furthermore, conditioned
  on the sampled plans, we implement a hierarchical parameterized trajectory generator
  with a refinement module to enhance prediction accuracy and a probability fusion
  strategy to boost prediction confidence. Extensive experimental results showcase
  our approach not only achieves state-of-the-art performance on the large-scale Argoverse
  & nuScenes motion forecasting benchmarks but also exhibits superior generalization
  abilities compared to existing supervised models.
