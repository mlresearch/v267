abstract: We introduce a Riemannian optimistic online learning algorithm for Hadamard
  manifolds based on inexact implicit updates. Unlike prior work, our method can handle
  in-manifold constraints, and matches the best known regret bounds in the Euclidean
  setting with no dependence on geometric constants, like the minimum curvature. Building
  on this, we develop algorithms for g-convex, g-concave smooth min-max problems on
  Hadamard manifolds. Notably, one method nearly matches the gradient oracle complexity
  of the lower bound for Euclidean problems, for the first time.
