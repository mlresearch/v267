title: 'Pruning for GNNs: Lower Complexity with Comparable Expressiveness'
openreview: 6AOnQ0KSUT
abstract: In recent years, the pursuit of higher expressive power in graph neural
  networks (GNNs) has often led to more complex aggregation mechanisms and deeper
  architectures. To address these issues, we have identified redundant structures
  in GNNs, and by pruning them, we propose Pruned MP-GNNs, K-Path GNNs, and K-Hop
  GNNs based on their original architectures. We show that 1) Although some structures
  are pruned in Pruned MP-GNNs and Pruned K-Path GNNs, their expressive power has
  not been compromised. 2) K-Hop MP-GNNs and their pruned architecture exhibit equivalent
  expressiveness on regular and strongly regular graphs. 3) The complexity of pruned
  K-Path GNNs and pruned K-Hop GNNs is lower than that of MP-GNNs, yet their expressive
  power is higher. Experimental results validate our refinements, demonstrating competitive
  performance across benchmark datasets with improved efficiency.
