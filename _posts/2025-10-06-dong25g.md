title: 'Discrepancies are Virtue: Weak-to-Strong Generalization through Lens of Intrinsic

abstract: 'Weak-to-strong (W2S) generalization is a type of finetuning (FT) where
  a strong (large) student model is trained on pseudo-labels generated by a weak teacher.
  Surprisingly, W2S FT often outperforms the weak teacher. We seek to understand this
  phenomenon through the observation that FT often occurs in intrinsically low-dimensional
  spaces. Leveraging the low intrinsic dimensionality of FT, we analyze W2S in the
  ridgeless regression setting from a variance reduction perspective. For a strong
  student-weak teacher pair with sufficiently expressive low-dimensional feature subspaces
  $\mathcal{V}_s, \mathcal{V}_w$, we provide an exact characterization of the variance
  that dominates the generalization error of W2S. This unveils a virtue of discrepancy
  between the strong and weak models in W2S: the variance of the weak teacher is inherited
  by the strong student in $\mathcal{V}_s \cap \mathcal{V}_w$, while reduced by a
  factor of $\mathrm{dim}(\mathcal{V}_s)/N$ in the subspace of discrepancy $\mathcal{V}_w
  \setminus \mathcal{V}_s$ with $N$ pseudo-labels for W2S. Our analysis further casts
  light on the sample complexities and the scaling of performance gap recovery in
  W2S. The analysis is supported by experiments on synthetic regression problems,
  as well as real vision and NLP tasks.'
