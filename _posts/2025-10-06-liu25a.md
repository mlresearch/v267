abstract: 'Graphics rendering applications increasingly leverage neural networks in
  tasks such as denoising, supersampling, and frame extrapolation to improve image
  quality while maintaining frame rates. The temporal coherence inherent in these
  tasks presents an opportunity to reuse intermediate results from previous frames
  and avoid redundant computations. Recent work has shown that caching intermediate
  features to be reused in subsequent inferences is an effective method to reduce
  latency in diffusion models. We extend this idea to real-time rendering and present
  ReFrame, which explores different caching policies to optimize trade-offs between
  quality and performance in rendering workloads. ReFrame can be applied to a variety
  of encoder-decoder style networks commonly found in rendering pipelines. Experimental
  results show that we achieve 1.4$\times$ speedup on average with negligible quality
  loss in three real-time rendering tasks. Code available: https://ubc-aamodt-group.github.io/reframe-layer-caching/'
