title: 'Weisfeiler and Leman Go Gambling: Why Expressive Lottery Tickets Win'

abstract: The lottery ticket hypothesis (LTH) is well-studied for convolutional neural
  networks but has been validated only empirically for graph neural networks (GNNs),
  for which theoretical findings are largely lacking. In this paper, we identify the
  expressivity of sparse subnetworks, i.e. their ability to distinguish non-isomorphic
  graphs, as crucial for finding winning tickets that preserve the predictive performance.
  We establish conditions under which the expressivity of a sparsely initialized GNN
  matches that of the full network, particularly when compared to the Weisfeiler-Leman
  test, and in that context put forward and prove a Strong Expressive Lottery Ticket
  Hypothesis. We subsequently show that an increased expressivity in the initialization
  potentially accelerates model convergence and improves generalization. Our findings
  establish novel theoretical foundations for both LTH and GNN research, highlighting
  the importance of maintaining expressivity in sparsely initialized GNNs. We illustrate
  our results using examples from drug discovery.
