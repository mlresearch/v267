title: 'GaussMarker: Robust Dual-Domain Watermark for Diffusion Models'
openreview: m7Mx14cxv8
abstract: As Diffusion Models (DM) generate increasingly realistic images, related
  issues such as copyright and misuse have become a growing concern. Watermarking
  is one of the promising solutions. Existing methods inject the watermark into the
  <em>single-domain</em> of initial Gaussian noise for generation, which suffers from
  unsatisfactory robustness. This paper presents the first <em>dual-domain</em> DM
  watermarking approach using a pipelined injector to consistently embed watermarks
  in both the spatial and frequency domains. To further boost robustness against certain
  image manipulations and advanced attacks, we introduce a model-independent learnable
  Gaussian Noise Restorer (GNR) to refine Gaussian noise extracted from manipulated
  images and enhance detection robustness by integrating the detection scores of both
  watermarks. GaussMarker efficiently achieves state-of-the-art performance under
  eight image distortions and four advanced attacks across three versions of Stable
  Diffusion with better recall and lower false positive rates, as preferred in real
  applications.
