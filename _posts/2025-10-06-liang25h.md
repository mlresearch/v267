title: 'ML$^2$-GCL: Manifold Learning Inspired Lightweight Graph Contrastive Learning'
openreview: irY40jSwCH
abstract: Graph contrastive learning has attracted great interest as a dominant and
  promising self-supervised representation learning approach in recent years. While
  existing works follow the basic principle of pulling positive pairs closer and pushing
  negative pairs far away, they still suffer from several critical problems, such
  as the underlying semantic disturbance brought by augmentation strategies, the failure
  of GCN in capturing long-range dependence, rigidness and inefficiency of node sampling
  techniques. To address these issues, we propose Manifold Learning Inspired Lightweight
  Graph Contrastive Learning (ML$^2$-GCL), which inherits the merits of both manifold
  learning and GCN. ML$^2$-GCL avoids the potential risks of semantic disturbance
  with only one single view. It achieves global nonlinear structure recovery from
  locally linear fits, which can make up for the defects of GCN. The most amazing
  advantage is about the lightweight due to its closed-form solution of positive pairs
  weights and removal of pairwise distances calculation. Theoretical analysis proves
  the existence of the optimal closed-form solution. Extensive empirical results on
  various benchmarks and evaluation protocols demonstrate effectiveness and lightweight
  of ML$^2$-GCL. We release the code at https://github.com/a-hou/ML2-GCL.
