abstract: 'We show that learning-rate schedules for large model training behave surprisingly
  similar to a performance bound from non-smooth convex optimization theory. We provide
  a bound for the constant schedule with linear cooldown; in particular, the practical
  benefit of cooldown is reflected in the bound due to the absence of logarithmic
  terms. Further, we show that this surprisingly close match between optimization
  theory and practice can be exploited for learning-rate tuning: we achieve noticeable
  improvements for training 124M and 210M Llama-type models by (i) extending the schedule
  for continued training with optimal learning-rate, and (ii) transferring the optimal
  learning-rate across schedules.'
