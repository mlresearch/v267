abstract: In recent years, the compression of large language models (LLMs) has emerged
  as a key problem in facilitating LLM deployment on resource-limited devices, reducing
  compute costs, and mitigating the environmental footprint due to large-scale AI
  infrastructure. Here, we establish the foundations of LLM quantization from a rate–distortion
  theory perspective and propose a quantization technique based on simple rate–distortion
  optimization. Our technique scales to models containing hundreds of billions of
  weight parameters and offers users the flexibility to compress models, post-training,
  to a model size or accuracy specified by the user.
