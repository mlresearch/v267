abstract: Millions of abandoned oil and gas wells are scattered across the world,
  leaching methane into the atmosphere and toxic compounds into the groundwater. Many
  of these locations are unknown, preventing the wells from being plugged and their
  polluting effects averted. Remote sensing is a relatively unexplored tool for pinpointing
  abandoned wells at scale. We introduce the first large-scale Benchmark dataset for
  this problem, leveraging high-resolution multi-spectral satellite imagery from Planet
  Labs. Our curated Dataset comprises over 213,000 wells (abandoned, suspended, and
  active) from Alberta, a region with especially high well density, sourced from the
  Alberta Energy Regulator and verified by domain experts. We evaluate baseline algorithms
  for well detection and segmentation, showing the promise of computer vision approaches
  but also significant room for improvement.
