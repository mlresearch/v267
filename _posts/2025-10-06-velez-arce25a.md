title: 'PyTDC: A multimodal machine learning training, evaluation, and inference platform
  for biomedical foundation models'
openreview: HV8vZDDoYc
abstract: Existing biomedical benchmarks do not provide end-to-end infrastructure
  for training, evaluation, and inference of models that integrate multimodal biological
  data and a broad range of machine learning tasks in therapeutics. We present PyTDC,
  an open-source machine-learning platform providing streamlined training, evaluation,
  and inference software for multimodal biological AI models. PyTDC unifies distributed,
  heterogeneous, continuously updated data sources and model weights and standardizes
  benchmarking and inference endpoints. This paper discusses the components of PyTDC’s
  architecture and, to our knowledge, the first-of-its-kind case study on the introduced
  single-cell drug-target nomination ML task. We find state-of-the-art methods in
  graph representation learning and domain-specific methods from graph theory perform
  poorly on this task. Though we find a context-aware geometric deep learning method
  that outperforms the evaluated SoTA and domain-specific baseline methods, the model
  is unable to generalize to unseen cell types or incorporate additional modalities,
  highlighting PyTDC’s capacity to facilitate an exciting avenue of research developing
  multimodal, context-aware, foundation models for open problems in biomedical AI.
