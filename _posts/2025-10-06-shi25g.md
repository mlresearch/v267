abstract: Accuracy and speed are critical in image editing tasks. Pan et al. introduced
  a drag-based framework using Generative Adversarial Networks, and subsequent studies
  have leveraged large-scale diffusion models. However, these methods often require
  over a minute per edit and exhibit low success rates. We present LightningDrag,
  which achieves high-quality drag-based editing in about one second on general images.
  By redefining drag-based editing as a conditional generation task, we eliminate
  the need for time-consuming latent optimization or gradient-based guidance. Our
  model is trained on large-scale paired video frames, capturing diverse motion (object
  translations, pose shifts, zooming, etc.) to significantly improve accuracy and
  consistency. Despite being trained only on videos, our model generalizes to local
  deformations beyond the training data (e.g., lengthening hair, twisting rainbows).
  Extensive evaluations confirm the superiority of our approach, and we will release
  both code and model.
