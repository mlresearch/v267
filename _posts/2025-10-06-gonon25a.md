title: A Rescaling-Invariant Lipschitz Bound Based on Path-Metrics for Modern ReLU
  Network Parameterizations
openreview: T8VLY1KuOz
abstract: Robustness with respect to weight perturbations underpins guarantees for
  generalization, pruning and quantization. Existing guarantees rely on <em>Lipschitz
  bounds in parameter space</em>, cover only plain feed-forward MLPs, and break under
  the ubiquitous neuron-wise rescaling symmetry of ReLU networks. We prove a new Lipschitz
  inequality expressed through the $\ell^{1}$-<em>path-metric</em> of the weights.
  The bound is (i) <em>rescaling-invariant</em> by construction and (ii) applies to
  any ReLU-DAG architecture with any combination of convolutions, skip connections,
  pooling, and frozen (inference-time) batch-normalization —thus encompassing ResNets,
  U-Nets, VGG-style CNNs, and more. By respecting the network’s natural symmetries,
  the new bound strictly sharpens prior parameter-space bounds and can be computed
  in two forward passes. To illustrate its utility, we derive from it a symmetry-aware
  pruning criterion and show—through a proof-of-concept experiment on a ResNet-18
  trained on ImageNet—that its pruning performance matches that of classical magnitude
  pruning, while becoming totally immune to arbitrary neuron-wise rescalings.
