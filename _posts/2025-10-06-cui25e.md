title: 'InfoSEM: A Deep Generative Model with Informative Priors for Gene Regulatory
  Network Inference'
openreview: bSrRCmx10g
abstract: Inferring Gene Regulatory Networks (GRNs) from gene expression data is crucial
  for understanding biological processes. While supervised models are reported to
  achieve high performance for this task, they rely on costly ground truth (GT) labels
  and risk learning gene-specific biases—such as class imbalances of GT interactions—rather
  than true regulatory mechanisms. To address these issues, we introduce InfoSEM,
  an unsupervised generative model that leverages textual gene embeddings as informative
  priors, improving GRN inference without GT labels. InfoSEM can also integrate GT
  labels as an additional prior when available, avoiding biases and further enhancing
  performance. Additionally, we propose a biologically motivated benchmarking framework
  that better reflects real-world applications such as biomarker discovery and reveals
  learned biases of existing supervised methods. InfoSEM outperforms existing models
  by 38.5% across four datasets using textual embeddings prior and further boosts
  performance by 11.1% when integrating labeled data as priors.
