abstract: In this work, we study reinforcement learning (RL) with trajectory feedback.
  Compared to the standard RL setting, in RL with trajectory feedback, the agent only
  observes the accumulative reward along the trajectory, and therefore, this model
  is particularly suitable for scenarios where querying the reward in each single
  step incurs prohibitive cost. For a finite-horizon Markov Decision Process (MDP)
  with $S$ states, $A$ actions and a horizon length of $H$, we develop an algorithm
  that enjoys an asymptotically nearly optimal regret of $\tilde{O}\left(\sqrt{SAH^3K}\right)$
  in $K$ episodes. To achieve this result, our new technical ingredients include (i)
  constructing a tighter confidence region for the reward function by incorporating
  the RL with trajectory feedback setting with techniques in linear bandits and (ii)
  constructing a reference transition model to better guide the exploration process.
