abstract: Looped Transformers provide advantages in parameter efficiency, computational
  capabilities, and generalization for reasoning tasks. However, their expressive
  power regarding function approximation remains underexplored. In this paper, we
  establish the approximation rate of Looped Transformers by defining the modulus
  of continuity for sequence-to-sequence functions. This reveals a limitation specific
  to the looped architecture. That is, the analysis prompts the incorporation of scaling
  parameters for each loop, conditioned on timestep encoding. Experiments validate
  the theoretical results, showing that increasing the number of loops enhances performance,
  with further gains achieved through the timestep encoding.
