title: A Generalization Theory for Zero-Shot Prediction

abstract: A modern paradigm for generalization in machine learning and AI consists
  of pre-training a task-agnostic foundation model, generally obtained using self-supervised
  and multimodal contrastive learning. The resulting representations can be used for
  prediction on a downstream task for which no labeled data is available. We present
  a theoretical framework to better understand this approach, called zero-shot prediction.
  We identify the target quantities that zero-shot prediction aims to learn, or learns
  in passing, and the key conditional independence relationships that enable its generalization
  ability.
