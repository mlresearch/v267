title: On the Convergence of Continuous Single-timescale Actor-critic
openreview: pV7hSmGJXP
abstract: Actor-critic algorithms have been instrumental in boosting the performance
  of numerous challenging applications involving continuous control, such as highly
  robust and agile robot motion control. However, their theoretical understanding
  remains largely underdeveloped. Existing analyses mostly focus on finite state-action
  spaces and on simplified variants of actor-critic, such as double-loop updates with
  i.i.d. sampling, which are often impractical for real-world applications. We consider
  the canonical and widely adopted single-timescale updates with Markovian sampling
  in continuous state-action space. Specifically, we establish finite-time convergence
  by introducing a novel Lyapunov analysis framework, which provides a unified convergence
  characterization of both the actor and the critic. Our approach is less conservative
  than previous methods and offers new insights into the coupled dynamics of actor-critic
  updates.
