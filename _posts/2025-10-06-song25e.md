title: Domain-Adapted Diffusion Model for PROTAC Linker Design Through the Lens of
  Density Ratio in Chemical Space
openreview: jkyUbkNJyH
abstract: Proteolysis-targeting chimeras (PROTACs) are a groundbreaking technology
  for targeted protein degradation, but designing effective linkers that connect two
  molecular fragments to form a drug-candidate PROTAC molecule remains a key challenge.
  While diffusion models show promise in molecular generation, current diffusion models
  for PROTAC linker design are typically trained on small molecule datasets, introducing
  distribution mismatches in the chemical space between small molecules and target
  PROTACs. Direct fine-tuning on limited PROTAC datasets often results in overfitting
  and poor generalization. In this work, we propose DAD-PROTAC, a domain-adapted diffusion
  model for PROTAC linker design, which addresses this distribution mismatch in chemical
  space through density ratio estimation to bridge the gap between small-molecule
  and PROTAC domains. By decomposing the target score estimator into a pre-trained
  score function and a lightweight score correction term, DAD-PROTAC achieves efficient
  fine-tuning without full retraining. Experimental results demonstrate its superior
  ability to generate high-quality PROTAC linkers.
