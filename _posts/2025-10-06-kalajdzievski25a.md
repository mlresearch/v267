abstract: The field of mechanistic interpretability in pre-trained transformer models
  has demonstrated substantial evidence supporting the ”linear representation hypothesis”,
  which is the idea that high level concepts are encoded as vectors in the space of
  activations of a model. Studies also show that model generation behavior can be
  steered toward a given concept by adding the concept’s vector to the corresponding
  activations. We show how to leverage these properties to build a form of logical
  implication into models, enabling transparent and interpretable adjustments that
  induce a chosen generation behavior in response to the presence of any given concept.
  Our method, Logical Implication Model Steering (LIMS), unlocks new hand-engineered
  reasoning capabilities by integrating neuro-symbolic logic into pre-trained transformer
  models.
