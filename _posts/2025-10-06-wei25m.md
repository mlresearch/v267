title: 'Open Your Eyes: Vision Enhances Message Passing Neural Networks in Link Prediction'

abstract: Message-passing graph neural networks (MPNNs) and structural features (SFs)
  are cornerstones for the link prediction task. However, as a common and intuitive
  mode of understanding, the potential of visual perception has been overlooked in
  the MPNN community. For the first time, we equip MPNNs with vision structural awareness
  by proposing an effective framework called Graph Vision Network (GVN), along with
  a more efficient variant (E-GVN). Extensive empirical results demonstrate that with
  the proposed frameworks, GVN consistently benefits from the vision enhancement across
  seven link prediction datasets, including challenging large-scale graphs. Such improvements
  are compatible with existing state-of-the-art (SOTA) methods and GVNs achieve new
  SOTA results, thereby underscoring a promising novel direction for link prediction.
