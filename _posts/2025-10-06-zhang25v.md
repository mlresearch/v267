abstract: In recent years, while generative AI has advanced significantly in image
  generation, video generation continues to face challenges in controllability, length,
  and detail quality, which hinder its application. We present MimicMotion, a framework
  for generating high-quality human videos of arbitrary length using motion guidance.
  Our approach has several highlights. Firstly, we introduce confidence-aware pose
  guidance that ensures high frame quality and temporal smoothness. Secondly, we introduce
  regional loss amplification based on pose confidence, which reduces image distortion
  in key regions. Lastly, we propose a progressive latent fusion strategy to generate
  long and smooth videos. Experiments demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach
  in producing high-quality human motion videos. Videos and comparisons are available
  at https://tencent.github.io/MimicMotion.
