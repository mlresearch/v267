title: Generalized additive models via direct optimization of regularized decision
  stump forests
openreview: 3WLpiPeJbk
abstract: We explore ensembles of axis-aligned decision stumps, which can be viewed
  as a generalized additive model (GAM). In this model, stumps utilizing the same
  feature are grouped to form a shape function for that feature. Instead of relying
  on boosting or bagging, we employ alternating optimization to learn a fixed-size
  stump forest. We optimize the parameters of each stump exactly through enumeration,
  given the other stumps are fixed. For fixed stump splits, the leaf values are optimized
  jointly by solving a convex problem. To address the overfitting issue inherent in
  naive optimization of stump forests, we propose effective regularization techniques.
  Our regularized stump forests achieve accuracy comparable to state-of-the-art GAM
  methods while using fewer parameters. This work is the first to successfully learn
  stump forests without employing traditional ensembling techniques like bagging or
  boosting.
