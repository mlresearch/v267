abstract: 'Causal reasoning and compositional reasoning are two core aspirations in
  AI. Measuring the extent of these behaviors requires principled evaluation methods.
  We explore a unified perspective that considers both behaviors simultaneously, termed
  <em>compositional causal reasoning</em> (CCR): the ability to infer how causal measures
  compose and, equivalently, how causal quantities propagate through graphs. We instantiate
  a framework for the systematic evaluation of CCR for the average treatment effect
  and the probability of necessity and sufficiency. As proof of concept, we demonstrate
  CCR evaluation for language models in the Llama, Phi, and GPT families. On a math
  word problem, our framework revealed a range of taxonomically distinct error patterns.
  CCR errors increased with the complexity of causal paths for all models except o1.'
