title: Contrastive Localized Language-Image Pre-Training
openreview: sGQEOXlezg
abstract: CLIP has been a celebrated method for training vision encoders to generate
  image/text representations facilitating various applications. Recently, it has been
  widely adopted as the vision backbone of multimodal large language models (MLLMs).
  The success of CLIP relies on aligning web-crawled noisy text annotations at image
  levels. However, such criteria may be insufficient for downstream tasks in need
  of fine-grained vision representations, especially when understanding region-level
  is demanding for MLLMs. We improve the localization capability of CLIP with several
  advances. Our proposed pre-training method, Contrastive Localized Language-Image
  Pre-training (CLOC), complements CLIP with region-text contrastive loss and modules.
  We formulate a new concept, promptable embeddings, of which the encoder produces
  image embeddings easy to transform into region representations given spatial hints.
  To support large-scale pre-training, we design a visually-enriched and spatially-localized
  captioning framework to effectively generate region-text labels. By scaling up to
  billions of annotated images, CLOC enables high-quality regional embeddings for
  recognition and retrieval tasks, and can be a drop-in replacement of CLIP to enhance
  MLLMs, especially on referring and grounding tasks.
