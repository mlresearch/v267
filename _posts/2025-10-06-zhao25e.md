title: Efficient Motion Prompt Learning for Robust Visual Tracking
openreview: aDVzd958YY
abstract: Due to the challenges of processing temporal information, most trackers
  depend solely on visual discriminability and overlook the unique temporal coherence
  of video data. In this paper, we propose a lightweight and plug-and-play motion
  prompt tracking method. It can be easily integrated into existing vision-based trackers
  to build a joint tracking framework leveraging both motion and vision cues, thereby
  achieving robust tracking through efficient prompt learning. A motion encoder with
  three different positional encodings is proposed to encode the long-term motion
  trajectory into the visual embedding space, while a fusion decoder and an adaptive
  weight mechanism are designed to dynamically fuse visual and motion features. We
  integrate our motion module into three different trackers with five models in total.
  Experiments on seven challenging tracking benchmarks demonstrate that the proposed
  motion module significantly improves the robustness of vision-based trackers, with
  minimal training costs and negligible speed sacrifice. Code is available at https://github.com/zj5559/Motion-Prompt-Tracking.
