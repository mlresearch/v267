abstract: This paper proposes a family of permutation-invariant graph embeddings,
  generalizing the Skew Spectrum of graphs of Kondor & Borgwardt (2008). Grounded
  in group theory and harmonic analysis, our method introduces a new class of graph
  invariants that are isomorphism-invariant and capable of embedding richer graph
  structures - including attributed graphs, multilayer graphs, and hypergraphs - which
  the Skew Spectrum could not handle. Our generalization further defines a family
  of functions that enables a trade-off between computational complexity and expressivity.
  By applying generalization-preserving heuristics to this family, we improve the
  Skew Spectrum’s expressivity at the same computational cost. We formally prove the
  invariance of our generalization, demonstrate its improved expressiveness through
  experiments, and discuss its efficient computation.
