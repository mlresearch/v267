title: Relational Conformal Prediction for Correlated Time Series
openreview: wwYDQ1vXcZ
abstract: We address the problem of uncertainty quantification in time series forecasting
  by exploiting observations at correlated sequences. Relational deep learning methods
  leveraging graph representations are among the most effective tools for obtaining
  point estimates from spatiotemporal data and correlated time series. However, the
  problem of exploiting relational structures to estimate the uncertainty of such
  predictions has been largely overlooked in the same context. To this end, we propose
  a novel distribution-free approach based on the conformal prediction framework and
  quantile regression. Despite the recent applications of conformal prediction to
  sequential data, existing methods operate independently on each target time series
  and do not account for relationships among them when constructing the prediction
  interval. We fill this void by introducing a novel conformal prediction method based
  on graph deep learning operators. Our approach, named Conformal Relational Prediction
  (CoRel), does not require the relational structure (graph) to be known a priori
  and can be applied on top of any pre-trained predictor. Additionally, CoRel includes
  an adaptive component to handle non-exchangeable data and changes in the input time
  series. Our approach provides accurate coverage and achieves state-of-the-art uncertainty
  quantification in relevant benchmarks.
