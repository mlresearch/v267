abstract: Motivated by applications in trajectory inference and particle tracking,
  we introduce <b>Smooth Schrödinger Bridges</b>. Our proposal generalizes prior work
  by allowing the reference process in the multi-marginal Schrödinger Bridge problem
  to be a smooth Gaussian process, leading to more regular and interpretable trajectories
  in applications. Though naïvely smoothing the reference process leads to a computationally
  intractable problem, we identify a class of processes (including the Matérn processes)
  for which the resulting Smooth Schrödinger Bridge problem can be <em>lifted</em>
  to a simpler problem on phase space, which can be solved in polynomial time. We
  develop a practical approximation of this algorithm that outperforms existing methods
  on numerous simulated and real single-cell RNAseq datasets.
