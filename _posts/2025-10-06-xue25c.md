abstract: 'While contrastive multi-view clustering has achieved remarkable success,
  it implicitly assumes balanced class distribution. However, real-world multi-view
  data primarily exhibits class imbalance distribution. Consequently, existing methods
  suffer performance degradation due to their inability to perceive and model such
  imbalance. To address this challenge, we present the first systematic study of imbalanced
  multi-view clustering, focusing on two fundamental problems: <em>i. perceiving class
  imbalance distribution</em>, and <em>ii. mitigating representation degradation of
  minority samples</em>. We propose PROTOCOL, a novel PaRtial Optimal TranspOrt-enhanced
  COntrastive Learning framework for imbalanced multi-view clustering. First, for
  class imbalance perception, we map multi-view features into a consensus space and
  reformulate the imbalanced clustering as a partial optimal transport (POT) problem,
  augmented with <em>progressive mass constraints</em> and <em>weighted KL divergence</em>
  for class distributions. Second, we develop a POT-enhanced class-rebalanced contrastive
  learning at both feature and class levels, incorporating <em>logit adjustment</em>
  and <em>class-sensitive learning</em> to enhance minority sample representations.
  Extensive experiments demonstrate that PROTOCOL significantly improves clustering
  performance on imbalanced multi-view data, filling a critical research gap in this
  field.'
