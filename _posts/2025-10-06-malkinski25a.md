abstract: 'Abstract visual reasoning (AVR) involves discovering shared concepts across
  images through analogy, akin to solving IQ test problems. Bongard Problems (BPs)
  remain a key challenge in AVR, requiring both visual reasoning and verbal description.
  We investigate whether multimodal large language models (MLLMs) can solve BPs by
  formulating a set of diverse MLLM-suited solution strategies and testing $4$ proprietary
  and $4$ open-access models on $3$ BP datasets featuring synthetic (classic BPs)
  and real-world (Bongard HOI and Bongard-OpenWorld) images. Despite some successes
  on real-world datasets, MLLMs struggle with synthetic BPs. To explore this gap,
  we introduce Bongard-RWR, a dataset representing synthetic BP concepts using real-world
  images. Our findings suggest that weak MLLM performance on classical BPs is not
  due to the domain specificity, but rather comes from their general AVR limitations.
  Code and dataset are available at: https://github.com/pavonism/bongard-rwr'
