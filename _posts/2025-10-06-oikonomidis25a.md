abstract: We analyze nonlinearly preconditioned gradient methods for solving smooth
  minimization problems. We introduce a generalized smoothness property, based on
  the notion of abstract convexity, that is broader than Lipschitz smoothness and
  provide sufficient first- and second-order conditions. Notably, our framework encapsulates
  algorithms associated with the gradient clipping method and brings out novel insights
  for the class of $(L_0,L_1)$-smooth functions that has received widespread interest
  recently, thus allowing us to extend beyond already established methods. We investigate
  the convergence of the proposed method in both the convex and nonconvex setting.
